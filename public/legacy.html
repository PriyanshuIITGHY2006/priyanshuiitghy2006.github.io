Research06
Working Paper · Jan – Feb 2026

BDH-ECHR: Post-Transformer Interpretable Legal AI for Multi-Label Violation Classification

Priyanshu Debnath · IIT Guwahati · Submitted to Kriti 2026 (High Prep Pathway AI)

Abstract

We apply the Dragon Hatchling (BDH) post-transformer architecture to multi-label classification of human rights violation articles in the European Court of Human Rights (ECHR) dataset. The BDH model achieves a Micro-F1 of 0.7716, outperforming BERT-base by 13.2 percentage points and LegalBERT by 5.2 points — at 5.4× fewer parameters (20.4M vs. 110M). We additionally conduct the first neuron→concept monosemanticity audit for legal AI: 218 neurons mapped to 477 legal concepts, with 2 neurons reaching perfect isolation (E=1.0). Per-document activation sparsity of 0.305% is 16× below BDH's own interpretability target. Hierarchical chunking extends effective context to 3,584 tokens — 7× BERT's standard window.
